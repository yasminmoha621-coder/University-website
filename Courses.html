Ours courses

The Department of Technology and Analytics offers a variety of courses designed to equip students with practical skills and knowledge in the technology field. Our courses include Information Technology, Data Science, Software Development, and Web Design and Development. These courses help students develop problem-solving, programming, data analysis, and computer skills. Our goal is to prepare students for successful careers in the modern digital world.

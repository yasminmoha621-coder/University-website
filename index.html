The Department of Technology and Analytics helps students learn about computers, technology, and database. It teaches students how to use modern technology to solve problems and improve their skills. The department prepares students for careers in the technology industry.

The department offers courses such as Web Design and Development, Database Systems, Computer Applications, and Analytics. These courses help students understand how websites are created, how information is used to make decision. students also learn practical computer skills.

Technology plays an important role in our daily lives. The department encourages students to be creative, solve problems, and work together. Students gain knowledge and skills that can help them find jobs and build successful careers in the world of technology
